# Gacha: styles, feels, kinds and auto fx

The reference for everything that makes Gacha a generator of *styles of music*, not just drum patterns. The tables below are generated from the code's own tables (`gacha_engine.STYLE_FEEL`, `gacha_section.RUNGS`, `gacha_autofx`, `gacha_midi.STYLE_VOICING`), so the numbers are the ones the engine uses. The manual is [README.md](README.md).

## How a section is made

Five choices stack up, each reaching further than the last:

| Layer | Where | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Style** | Generate tab, *Style*; Perform tab, Shapes row, *style* | The music's style, the feel: everything the kit's pattern does not reach: how many textures and how long they loop, reversed or stretched, their effect chains, where the chops land and from how many slices, how many one-shots and how long, the swing, the fills, the rolls, the sub, the racks' auto fx; and its own kit unless the drums box says another. *random* draws one per song or section, *default* is the engine before the feels (the knobs alone). |
| **Drums** | next to the style, *drums*; Perform tab, *drums* | The kit's pattern alone: where the kick, snare, hats and ride hit on the 16-step grid, the open hats, the synth kit's flavour. *same as style* (default) plays the style's own kit, *random* draws one, or any kit under any style. |
| **Kind** | Perform tab, *next kind* | The section's density: sparse, groove, then the ladder dense, heavy, insane, overload (the feel turned up), for the layers, chops and events (and the drums too with *drum kind* on *same as style*), plus break, build, intro, outro. |
| **Drum kind** | Perform tab, *drum kind* beside *next kind* | The drums' and the sub's own rung (hats, ghosts, rolls, stutters, fills, the doubled kick; break, build and sparse drop or stack the voices): *same as style* (default), *random*, or any of the eight. The MIDI out plays what the drums play, and auto fx m:c scales with it. |
| **Auto fx** | Perform tab, *auto fx*, *auto fx drums* and *auto fx m:c* | The racks for the style: knobs set as the section lands, LFOs on the slow movers, and moves in time (a delay thrown on an 8th, the codec at full for a 16th, the ladder closing over the last bars); the third tick moves the Model:Cycles' own sound and retrigs over MIDI. |
| **Style voicing** | M:C tab, MIDI out row | The MIDI out's hits pitched by style, for a chromatic receiver. |

Every feel bends the Generate tab's knob of the same matter and never replaces it: a multiplier on its chance, a range inside its range. A knob at 0 stays 0 in every style, and a swing set by hand wins.

## The kinds: a ladder of density

Sparse is one voice and one layer, groove the full kit under one to three layers and maybe chops. Above groove every rung is the current feel turned up, so an insane hypnotic section is still hypnotic. Break is one or two voices under more layers, build the voices stacking up bar by bar; intro and outro follow their own styles. *random* draws among groove, break, build, sparse, dense, heavy, insane, overload.

## Auto fx: the stems' racks

With *auto fx* ticked, every section that lands on its bar (and a song started through the racks) has the four stems' racks (drums bus, layers, chops, events) reset, then three things drawn per section, each line with its chance in brackets, so two sections in one style differ:

* **Set**: a knob held for the section, the amount drawn from its range.
* **LFO**: a knob moved on the live clock, put in an LFO slot that is off (never one you turned on) and taken back before the next section. ± moves it either way, + upward only.
* **Moves**: an effect switched on at a place in the loop and off after its length (a hold, a rise or a fall), run by the audio engine block by block on the player's own position, so it lands within one audio block (about 3 ms) of its grid line; several at once, the largest wins, and the knob's own setting takes over again after. The Audio FX grid lights the cell and shows the move's amount, the setting untouched. A knob an LFO holds is left to the LFO.

The rack knobs run 0 to 1, fully wet at 1; the pitch and pan knobs are -1 to 1 (down, left negative). A pan move wins over the knob while it is on (the effects take the larger of the two). A knob gets one LFO at most (the style's rows first, then the palette below), and a move on a knob an LFO holds is left out.

## Auto fx m:c: the Model:Cycles

*auto fx m:c* moves the box's own sound over CC on the MIDI out, for the style, drawn per section from its seed: its tracks' decay, color, shape, sweep, contour, pan, delay and reverb sends, chance, punch and gate, its delay time and feedback, its reverb size and tone, and the retrig done from here, each with its rate, its velocity curve's length, its fade and its velocity. A track is named by the drum it plays (kick T1, snare T2, hihat T3, ohat T4, ride T5, glitch T6), fx is the delay and reverb. The M:C tab's values are the centre: a +/- amount is that far from the cell's value, a plain range is set as it is; a move goes back to the centre after (a rising one climbs to its amount by the end). The cells themselves are never changed (one the moves hold elsewhere lights up and shows what the box gets, as on the Audio FX grid), a cell an LFO holds is left alone, and the pitch is never touched (the box glides a CC jump into a portamento, and it sounded wrong). The moves run in the audio engine on the section's loop, ahead like the hits, and switch with the section on its bar; a retrig plays only while its drum is heard and never over one held by hand. Unticked or the section stopped, the box gets the tab's values back.

The drum kind playing scales the odds and the throws' counts: intro x0.5, sparse x0.5, break x0.7, build x0.8, outro x0.8, groove x1, dense x1.3, heavy x1.6, insane x2, overload x2.6; a build always adds the snare's roll into the drop.

## Style voicing on the MIDI out

With *style voicing* ticked (M:C tab), the hits leave the map's note by some semitones per style, for a receiver whose drums play chromatically (the Model:Cycles' tracks, a sampler); the whole kit is also moved into the section's key. Styles not listed send the map as is.

| Style | What the voicing does |
|---|---|
| `hypnotic` | the pulse (snare) climbs the harmonic series over the bar, a harmonic per eighth: Tenney's pulse rate as pitch, the rising figure the pattern's ramp only hints at (the fifth harmonic a minor third in a minor key) |
| `footwork` | the cells' kicks off the beat a fourth up in the first half of the bar, a fifth in the second: the kick as toms |
| `halftime` | every kick but the one a fourth down: the 808's slide |
| `dembow` | the figure's lighter hits (steps 3 and 11) a fourth up |
| `clave` | the clave on two blocks: the 3 side an octave up, the 2 side a fifth |
| `breakbeat` | the snare's ghosts (under half velocity) three semitones down, the sampler's trick |
| `dnb` | the snare's ghosts three semitones down, as in breakbeat |
| `ukg` | the skippy 16th hats (the odd steps) a third up, the key's own (major or minor) |
| `one-drop` | the rim (the snare at full) a fifth up, the ghost as it is |
| `idm` | the kick and the snare on a pitch of their own per step, fixed for the song |
| `glitch` | as idm: the kick and the snare on a pitch of their own per step |
| `dub` | as one-drop: the rim (the snare at full) a fifth up |
| `polyrhythm` | the cross-rhythm's hits between the four's beats (the snare off the beat) a fourth up, the ones on a beat as they are |
| `electro` | the kick's off-the-beat hits (the 3-3-2 figure) a fourth up, as footwork |
| `amapiano` | the kick's off-the-beat hits an octave up: the log drum |

## Where to tune it

| To change | Edit |
|---|---|
| a style's kit pattern | `gacha_engine.make_pattern`, its `elif style == ...` branch; open hats in `OPEN_HAT` |
| a style's feel | its row in `gacha_engine.STYLE_FEEL` (fields documented on `Feel`) |
| a rung of the ladder | its row in `gacha_section.RUNGS` (fields documented on `Rung`) |
| the stems' auto fx | `gacha_autofx.AUTO_FX`, `AUTO_LFO`, `AUTO_GESTURES` |
| the voices' auto fx | `gacha_autofx.AUTO_FX_VOICES`, `AUTO_LFO_VOICES`, `AUTO_GESTURES_VOICES` |
| the Model:Cycles' auto fx | `gacha_autofx.MC_GENERIC`, `AUTO_MC`, `MC_INTENSITY` |
| the MIDI voicing | `gacha_midi.STYLE_VOICING` and `style_offset` |
| a new style | all of the above, plus `DRUM_STYLES` and `SYNTH_FLAVOUR` in gacha_engine, the Drum style list in gacha.py and its README row |
