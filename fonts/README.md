# Fonts

Faces used by the word-cloud video effect: terminal and typewriter style
monospace fonts, all from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(see `OFL.txt`): VT323, Share Tech Mono, Space Mono, IBM Plex Mono,
JetBrains Mono, Inconsolata, Courier Prime, Major Mono Display, Cutive Mono.
Drop any other `.ttf` or `.otf` in here and it joins the pool at the next
launch.
